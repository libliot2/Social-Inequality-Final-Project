# 进入精英大学之后，留学机会仍然不平等吗？

[Access 模块](access.md) · [调研原稿及归属](../evidence/source-registry.md#sr12) · [交接状态](../research-status.md)

2017 届北大受访毕业生中，家庭职业阶层与获得海外研究生 offer 有关。同一所大学提供了观察校内差距的场景。北京多校的纵向调查进一步显示，家庭背景的关联在申请阶段已经出现，在已申请者的获录取阶段也可见。这里讨论的是本科毕业后海外深造的选择机会，研究意义在于理解高等教育如何影响向上流动所需的后续教育机会。

## 四页展示稿

以下内容可直接复制到小组幻灯片。英文段落可作口播，建议总时长约 3–4 分钟。

### 第 1 页 · Does entering the same university level the playing field?

**投影片**　Elite university admission opens doors. Do students from different family backgrounds have comparable opportunities to pursue postgraduate study abroad?

**讲解**　Getting into an elite university is often seen as a route to upward mobility. Students at the same university gain access to many of the same academic resources. Our question is whether they can turn those resources into similar opportunities after graduation. We focus on who can consider, apply for and obtain an overseas postgraduate offer.

### 第 2 页 · Overseas study is an established graduation pathway

**投影片**　18.4% of Tsinghua’s 2024 undergraduate graduates were reported as pursuing further study outside mainland China. Beijing Jiaotong University reported 625 of 3,840 graduates (16.28%) in its own reporting cohort.

**讲解**　These figures show why overseas postgraduate education is worth studying. A substantial share of graduates at these universities pursue further study outside mainland China. Each percentage comes from the university’s own graduation statistics. To see whether family background shapes access within a university, we need data that record both family background and students’ outcomes.

**页脚**　Tsinghua University, 2024 cohort · Beijing Jiaotong University, 2024 cohort. [E80](../evidence/evidence-master.md#e80) · [E82](../evidence/evidence-master.md#e82)

### 第 3 页 · Family background and overseas offers at Peking University

**投影片主图数据**　用 0–100% 的成对横条比较两个群体。两列分别以全体受访者和获至少一个海外 offer 的受访者为分母。

| Family occupational background | All respondents | Overseas offer recipients |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Cadre, managerial or professional | 69.7% | 86.5% |
| Urban working-class or agricultural-worker | 18.6% | 7.3% |
| Unspecified | 11.7% | 6.2% |

**讲解**　Shen and colleagues surveyed 1,417 Peking University graduates in 2017. Students from cadre, managerial or professional families made up about 70 percent of respondents and 87 percent of those who obtained an overseas offer. Students from urban working-class or agricultural-worker families made up about 19 percent of respondents and 7 percent of offer recipients. In a model that included GPA and other observed characteristics, a one-level increase in self-reported family income corresponded to 1.755 times the odds of receiving an offer. The survey had a 46.1 percent response rate, so these figures describe the respondents in this cohort.

**页脚**　Shen et al. (2022), Tables 1–3. 69.7% 等为原表类别加总。 [E51](../evidence/evidence-master.md#e51) · [E20](../evidence/evidence-master.md#e20)

### 第 4 页 · Where does the gap appear?

**投影片**　15 Beijing universities · 1,926 students followed → 277 applied abroad → 251 received an offer → 216 enrolled.

**讲解**　Hu and colleagues found that family socioeconomic status was positively associated with both applying and receiving an offer among applicants. Among students who already held an offer, their analysis found no statistically significant association with enrollment. In a separate analysis of the same Beijing survey program, Li found that family income and parental education were associated with the choice of overseas versus domestic postgraduate study. This brings us to the question for the next section. How might money, information and earlier international experiences shape which options students can pursue?

**页脚**　Hu et al. (2020), §§3.1, 4.1, 4.3 · Li (2016), Tables 2 and 7. [E76](../evidence/evidence-master.md#e76) · [E77](../evidence/evidence-master.md#e77) · [E78](../evidence/evidence-master.md#e78) · [E79](../evidence/evidence-master.md#e79) · [E61](../evidence/evidence-master.md#e61)
